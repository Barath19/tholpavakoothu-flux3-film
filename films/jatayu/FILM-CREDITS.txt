JATAYU: THE LAST FLIGHT
An original AI-assisted cinematic adaptation for Barath's hackathon project.

STORY
Adapted from the Ramayana: Jatayu confronts Ravana, falls, and survives long
enough to identify Sita's captor to Rama. The English captions and closing
line are original writing, not quotations from scripture.
https://www.valmiki.iitk.ac.in/sloka?field_kanda_tid=3&field_sarga_value=51&language=dv
https://valmikiramayan.net/aranya/sarga68/aranyasans68.htm

ORIGINAL SOUNDTRACK
Newly composed and synthesized for this film: percussion and reed score,
ambient drone, bronze-gong-like tones, wingbeats and wood/leather impacts.
Created using deterministic modal and noise synthesis; no third-party recorded
music samples. FLUX 3 generated clip audio is mixed quietly underneath.
Instrumentation research: Tholpavakoothu Puppet Centre, Koonathara, Kerala.
https://tholpavakoothu.in/about-us/about-performance/
This is a Kerala theatre-inspired score, not a traditional troupe recording.

GENERATION AND EDIT
FLUX 3 Image Klein Distilled + custom Tholpavakoothu style LoRA (step 2000).
LoRA training on Nebius compute; motion and native audio through FLUX 3 Video.
Editing, captions, original score and sound mix: Python, NumPy, Pillow, FFmpeg.
